Place the official, approved Blair Academy seal file here as:

    blair_seal.png

Once it's here, the app will automatically display it in the sidebar,
the main header, and as the window/tab icon.

Why this folder is empty by default:
Blair's Graphic & Editorial Style Guide states that the Blair seal is a
registered copyright and "BLAIR ACADEMY" is a registered trademark —
any use requires a request to the Director of Communications. This app
doesn't include or redraw that artwork itself; it's built to display
the real, approved file once you have it.

Recommended source: a PNG or SVG export of the "seal with no ribbon"
version (per the style guide, page 4), on a transparent background,
at least 200x200px.
